Gian mascot — design exploration

Four original concepts for a mascot that doubles as Gian's logomark and as the "session is working" indicator. Each is shown statically and as an animated loop, at 16 / 32 / 96 px. Toggle theme + accent to confirm they re-tone correctly.

1. Boombox-G
letterform · loud · logo-ready

A bold slab-serif G with an open mouth in its notch — it sings, it shouts, it broadcasts. Pure logomark: no character body, so no chance of likeness collision.

Why it works — Reads as a logo at 16 px (just a confident G silhouette). At larger sizes the eyebrow furrow and singing mouth give it attitude. Nothing here resembles a cartoon character — it's a letterform with a mood. Animation: 1.6 s gentle bob + 0.7 s mouth pulse + 1.4 s sound rings on offset delays so the loop boundary disappears.

3. Bolt-Kid
character · running cycle · most "mascot-y"

An original chunky character — round dumpling body, spiky tuft, sweatband, angry V brows. Deliberately different shape language than Doraemon's Gian: no striped shirt (plain accent body), no pageboy haircut (a spiky tuft), not a circle head (a softer rectangle). Same "tough little kid" energy.

Why it works — Highest personality density at 96 px. Shape language deliberately diverges from the comic Gian: dumpling torso (not long-shirt rectangle), spiky tuft (not bowl-cut), rounded-rect head (not circle). At 16 px detail collapses into colored blobs — this works best as the showcase / empty-state hero, not the favicon. Animation: arms + legs swing on a 0.55 s cycle with a tiny body hop; dust puffs trail behind.
